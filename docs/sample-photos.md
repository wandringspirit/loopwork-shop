# Sample photos

The 15 sample products use free photos from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com) under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license). They're placeholders only. Customers must see your own pieces, so replace every photo before launch.

To replace a product's photos: put your own photos in its folder, update the `photos` list in its `index.md`, and remove `sample: true`. A production build (`SHOP_ENV=production`) refuses to publish while any product still has `sample: true`.
