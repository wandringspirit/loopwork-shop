We only ask for what we need to make and deliver your order.

## What we collect

When you send an order request or a message, we receive:

- your name and WhatsApp number
- your email address, if you give it
- your delivery address and PIN code
- the pieces you ordered and any notes you add

## How we use it

To confirm your order, arrange payment and delivery, and reply to your messages. We don't sell your details, and we don't send marketing messages unless you ask for them.

## Where it's kept

Your messages stay in our WhatsApp. Order details are kept in our private order records so we can track and deliver your order.

## Your cart

Your cart is saved in your own browser so it's still there when you come back. Nothing is sent to us until you send an order request.

## Your choices

Ask us on WhatsApp or by email to see, correct or delete the details we hold about you.
