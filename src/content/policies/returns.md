Every piece is made by hand in small numbers, so returns work a little differently from a big shop.

## Cancelling an order

- You can cancel for free any time **before you pay**. Just tell us on WhatsApp.
- After payment, ready-to-ship orders can be cancelled for a full refund until they're dispatched.
- Made-to-order and custom pieces can't be cancelled once we've started making them.

## Returns

We don't accept returns for a change of mind, or for the small variations in colour and size that come with handmade work.

## Damaged or wrong items

If your piece arrives damaged or isn't what you ordered, message us on WhatsApp **within 48 hours of delivery** with photos. We'll repair, replace or refund it.

## Refunds

Approved refunds go back to the account you paid from, usually within 7 working days.
