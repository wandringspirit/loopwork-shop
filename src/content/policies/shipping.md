## Where we ship

We ship across India. If you're outside India, message us on WhatsApp and we'll see what's possible.

## When your order leaves

- **Ready-to-ship pieces** are packed and sent within 3 working days of payment.
- **Made-to-order and custom pieces** are sent as soon as they're finished. Each product page shows the usual making time, and we confirm it with you before you pay.

## How long delivery takes

Most parcels arrive 4–7 working days after dispatch. Remote areas can take a little longer. We'll message you on WhatsApp when your parcel is on its way.

## Shipping charges

Charges depend on your PIN code and the size of your parcel. We work them out after you send your order request, and confirm the total on WhatsApp before you pay.

## Damaged parcels

If your parcel arrives damaged, message us within 48 hours with photos of the parcel and the piece. See our [returns policy](/policies/returns/) for what happens next.
