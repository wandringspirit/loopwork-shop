## Orders

Sending an order request doesn't commit you to buy. Your order is confirmed once we've agreed the details on WhatsApp and received your payment.

## Prices

Prices are in Indian rupees (₹). Shipping is added separately and confirmed before you pay. Prices on the website can change, but the total we confirm with you on WhatsApp is the one you pay.

## Handmade pieces

Every piece is made by hand, so small differences in size, shape and colour are part of its character. Colours can also look different from screen to screen.

## Custom orders

We agree the design, price and timeline with you before we start. Custom pieces are made just for you, so they can't be cancelled or returned once work begins, unless they arrive damaged.
